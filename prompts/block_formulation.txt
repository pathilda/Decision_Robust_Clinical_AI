FORMAL PROBLEM
The caseload assignment is solved as the following optimization problem.

Notation
- I: the set of new clients in the group. J: the set of clinicians.
- s_i: the complexity of new client i, determined by its assessment.{SCORE_NOTE}
- L0_j: the current caseload complexity of clinician j.
- cap_j: the maximum number of new clients clinician j can take.
- x_ij = 1 if client i is assigned to clinician j, and 0 otherwise.

Final caseload of clinician j:
  L_j = L0_j + sum over i in I of s_i * x_ij

Objective (in words): make the clinicians' final caseloads as equal as possible, by minimizing the difference between the largest and the smallest final caseload.
Objective (formally):
  minimize   max over j in J of L_j  -  min over j in J of L_j

Constraints
(1) In words: every new client is assigned to exactly one clinician.
    Formally: sum over j in J of x_ij = 1, for every i in I.
(2) In words: no clinician receives more new clients than their limit.
    Formally: sum over i in I of x_ij <= cap_j, for every j in J.
(3) In words: each assignment is either made or not made.
    Formally: x_ij in {0, 1}.
